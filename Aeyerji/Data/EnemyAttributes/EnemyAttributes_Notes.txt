M_GruntAttrs
Intent: Baseline melee foot soldier for packs and early encounters.
Notes: Balanced speed and damage, low armor, short reach; good tuning baseline.
Use: Filler enemies or wave padding around elites.

M_BruteAttrs
Intent: Slow, heavy bruiser that anchors the front line.
Notes: Very high HP and armor, short melee reach, heavy single hits.
Use: Force players to kite or burn down a sturdy target.

M_SkirmisherAttrs
Intent: Fast harasser that darts in and out.
Notes: Low HP, high run speed, quick attacks, low damage per hit.
Use: Pressure backlines and punish slow players.

M_BerserkerAttrs
Intent: Aggressive damage dealer that snowballs if ignored.
Notes: High attack speed and damage, low armor, moderate HP.
Use: Glass cannon melee threat.

M_TankAttrs
Intent: Damage sponge that blocks lanes.
Notes: Extreme HP/armor, very slow, low damage.
Use: Soak player bursts while other enemies attack.

M_AssassinAttrs
Intent: Hit-and-run burst assassin.
Notes: Very high speed, narrow attack arc, low HP, high DPS spikes.
Use: Flanks and deletes isolated targets.

M_BrawlerAttrs
Intent: Crowd brawler with wide swings.
Notes: Moderate HP/armor, wide attack angle, steady pace.
Use: Disrupts groups and pins players.

M_GuardianAttrs
Intent: Defensive escort that protects key units.
Notes: High armor, moderate HP, slower attacks, reliable reach.
Use: Bodyguard for casters or bosses.

M_DuelistAttrs
Intent: Precision melee fighter focused on single targets.
Notes: Fast attacks, narrow angle, moderate damage, agile.
Use: Dangerous in 1v1, weaker vs groups.

M_CleaverAttrs
Intent: Heavy sweeping attacker for crowd control.
Notes: Wide attack arc, high damage per hit, slow cooldown.
Use: Punish clumped players.

M_ReaverAttrs
Intent: Relentless chaser with sustained pressure.
Notes: Above-average damage, moderate speed and armor.
Use: Keeps pressure on fleeing targets.

M_PackHunterAttrs
Intent: Swarm unit that overwhelms by volume.
Notes: Very fast, very low HP, extremely high attack speed, low damage.
Use: Comes in packs; dangerous only in groups.

M_EnforcerAttrs
Intent: Reliable midline bruiser.
Notes: Solid HP/armor, dependable damage, moderate speed.
Use: General-purpose melee threat.

M_BulwarkAttrs
Intent: Fortress unit that refuses to die.
Notes: Extreme HP/armor, very slow, minimal damage.
Use: Forces repositioning and target prioritization.

M_VanguardAttrs
Intent: Aggressive spearhead that starts engagements.
Notes: Fast approach, good damage, moderate armor, larger patrol.
Use: Leads packs and closes distance quickly.

R_RangedAttrs
Intent: Baseline ranged shooter for general encounters.
Notes: Balanced range and damage, moderate speed, standard projectile speed.
Use: Default ranged pack member.

R_CasterAttrs
Intent: Spellcaster backliner with ability focus.
Notes: High mana and range, slower attacks, fragile armor.
Use: Ability-driven threat behind melee screen.

R_SniperAttrs
Intent: Long-range precision threat.
Notes: Very high range and damage, slow attack cadence, low HP.
Use: Punishes stationary targets.

R_BombardierAttrs
Intent: Area pressure artillery.
Notes: Wide attack angle, high damage, slow projectiles, moderate HP.
Use: Controls space and punishes clustering.

R_SupportAttrs
Intent: Utility backliner that enables others.
Notes: Higher mana and armor, low damage, faster cooldowns.
Use: Buff, debuff, or heal support style.
